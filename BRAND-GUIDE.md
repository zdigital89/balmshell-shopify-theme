# Balmshell Beauty Brand Guide

## Brand Personality
Balmshell Beauty is retro clean beauty with bold lip-gloss energy: playful, confident, feminine, warm, slightly sexy, and handmade. Cute but elevated. Clean, not cluttered.

## Fonts
- Headline / bubble display: Fredoka, weights 600-700.
- Body / product copy: Inter, weights 500, 700, 800.
- Fallback stack: Fredoka, Inter, system-ui, sans-serif.

## Visual Rules
- Backgrounds stay cream or cream-light.
- Cards use 8px radius, dark olive borders, and soft shadows.
- Buttons are pill-shaped with blush or cream fill, dark olive border, thick dark olive drop shadow, and glossy white highlight mark.
- Product images should feel like sticker cutouts with blush, cream, and olive outline layers.
- Use real product photos first. Avoid cluttered decorative overlays.
- Use minimal stars, gloss marks, lips, and shimmer details.

## Product Families
- Soaps
- Shimmer Lotion Bars
- Lip Balms
